## 🌟 About M-Mart

**M-Mart** is a multi-service application designed to bring everyday local
services into one convenient platform.

The application focuses on two main services:

- 🛍️ **Grocery Shopping**
- 🚗 **Local Transport Booking**

Customers can purchase grocery products, manage their cart, place orders,
and book available transport services from the same application.

An **Admin Dashboard** will provide centralized management of products,
orders, transport bookings, and drivers.
